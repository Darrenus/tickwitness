# ADR0049: One corrected EC2 qualification retaining two failed attempts

Date: 2026-11-27 Asia/Singapore. Status: accepted for the bounded internal
reference after independent static review and actual freeze push/readback;
not product M1 acceptance or an instance-Start bypass.

## Problem

The second real AWS attempt stopped at independent process-limit readback before
GO. Linux field padding was subsequently found incompatible with the matcher;
the corrected validator, raw refusal capture and real procfs regressions passed
remote source checks. The missing original raw limits prevent establishing the
sole cause, and neither source CI nor the repair reopens consumed recovery v1.
The owner now explicitly requests corrected-source validation on actual AWS,
remote-only tests, automatic Stop and external-human reproduction still PENDING.

## Decision

Freeze [one-use recovery v2](../contracts/restricted-reference-ec2-recovery-v2.md)
before implementation. Add one fixed third technical invocation only, retaining
the original and recovery-v1 failures, their two charges and actual immutable
records. Give this invocation at most24 original controls,600s and30s setup;
cumulative cap26. All24 must independently pass on one new actual source/boot.
Original research budgets are unchanged and no research reservation/replay occurs.

Use separate fixed v2 plan/consumption/Stop paths, kinds and integer schema2,
an explicit v2 factory and exact CLI dispatch. A fixed two-slot history verifies
both old runs and the still-used v1 plan/consumption, including their original
lineage and accounting phases. Hold the same original read-only host-claim flock.
Durably consume v2 once before new run/metadata/policy. Old paths, v1 shapes and
refusal behavior remain unchanged; no recursive or automatic fourth recovery.

Retain exact controls, independent observations and owned cleanup. Preserve raw
limits on failure and keep GO behind every original gate. A complete new report,
durable final SHA and re-read receipt must bind current24 plus historical2 and all
actual same-source case bytes/tickets/outcomes. Uncertainty grants no fresh count.

## Consequences

This narrowly fixed successor is more explicit than a generic retry ledger but
does not weaken exhausted-host refusal. Root owner, repository, provisioner and
OS remain trusted; owner Stop records and private archive/index hashes have their
stated limited provenance. The instance stays stopped during source work and
starts only with a concrete reviewed package and the existing access procedure.

Every actual outcome preserves bounded evidence and ends with automatic Stop
plus independent AWS readback. Even full reference success keeps product M1
DISABLED, PR115 Draft/#38 incomplete, human security review NOT_PERFORMED and
external-human reproduction0/0/PENDING until those distinct gates pass.
