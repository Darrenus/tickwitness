## Review and outcome vocabulary

Keep independent dimensions rather than one success flag:

| Dimension | Examples of explicit states | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participation | UNREVIEWED / NEEDS_EVIDENCE / VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_HUMAN / MAINTAINER / AUTOMATION | Actual human participation and operator attribution, reviewed separately from self-reported role |
| Technical attempt | NOT_RUN / SUCCESS / FAILED / REFUSED / UNKNOWN | Actual commands/environment/receipts; preserve raw failures and uncertainty |
| Own-case fit | UNKNOWN / SUPPORTED / CAPABILITY_GAP / NOT_OWN_CASE | Participant's research and lawful inputs, supported profile and declared baseline/constraints |
| Activation review | PENDING / ELIGIBLE / NOT_ELIGIBLE | Real external own research completed baseline, declared constraints and valid complete result; named human reviewer/date/refs |
| Research decision | UNKNOWN / PARTICIPANT_CONFIRMED | Actual participant choice and reason; a specific no-change decision is allowed |
| Reuse observation | NOT_STARTED / WINDOW_OPEN / WINDOW_MATURE / WITHDRAWN / UNKNOWN | Real activation date, separate self-initiated research and observation dates |

ELIGIBLE is a reviewed adoption assessment, not an executable system state or product-authority token. Missing identity/consent/operation/result evidence remains PENDING/NEEDS_EVIDENCE, not automatically eligible. A verified external human's installation failure can be a real attempt but not activation. SDK-only technical SUCCESS can correctly coexist with ACTIVATION_PENDING. Official examples, CI/AI/owner proxy runs and role-only assertions never increase verified activation/reuse counts.

Activation retains the original definition: own research, baseline, declared constraints and a valid complete result, which may honestly be scoped not-found or complete evidence-insufficient. Raw crashes, unsupported execution or incomplete/unfinalized output do not become complete results by renaming them. Value realization separately requires independent reproduction AND a participant-confirmed research decision; software-generated advice is not that decision.

Only a real reviewed activation starts that participant's 30-day window. A self-initiated different research on day 5 may be recorded as a reuse event, but the full 30-day observation is still immature. Same-case reruns, official demo repeats, owner-operated work and future intentions are not reuse. Never start a window at an SDK/PR merge date. Reports retain denominator, cohort/window, source and missing values; no activation dates means no calculable reuse rate. Original Beta/GA human-adoption thresholds remain those in the adoption plan, not newly achieved milestones.
